## 5. Needs subject-expert review

- t11 geological timeline: era dates 541-252, 252-66 and 66-0 million years ago are standard geological values but are NOT in the lesson text. Claim to check: Palaeozoic about 541-252 Ma, Mesozoic about 252-66 Ma, Cenozoic about 66 Ma to present.
- t11 geological timeline: the cards put "algae, then bryophytes and pteridophytes" and "first reptiles" under Palaeozoic and "first birds and mammals" under Mesozoic, copied from the lesson table. Confirm they match the NCERT-level expectation.
- t11 Miller figure: apparatus drawn as boiling water flask, spark flask, condenser and trap; the lesson gives only the gases, spark and amino acid result (it says 800 degrees Celsius, which is not shown). Check the layout is acceptable as a schematic.
- t11 natural selection curves: the stabilising curve is drawn higher and narrower with the same total area; the disruptive curve is two equal peaks. Check that equal total number of individuals before and after is acceptable.
- t10 Bt figure and callout: "cry gene put into cotton, plant makes inactive protoxin, activated in alkaline gut". Check that stating the plant makes the protoxin form (rather than the activated toxin) agrees with the intended answer.
- t10 insulin figure: "A and B chains made in separate E. coli (plasmids)" and "51 amino acids". The lesson states the chains were produced separately; check that showing them as two parallel bacterial flows is acceptable.
- t10 gene therapy: the side box links permanent cure to introducing the ADA gene into "cells at an early embryonic stage", following the lesson wording.
- t12 species-area graph: both lines share the same intercept C (illustrative) to compare slopes; Z = 1.0 was picked as a value inside the lesson's 0.6-1.2 range for continents. Check that showing two lines in one log-log graph is acceptable.
- t12 hotspot figure: states 34 hotspots and the three covering India, repeated from the lesson text ("the figure given in NCERT"). Check the count and the names against the current NCERT edition.
- t13 contraception grid: dots are assigned from the lesson text. Claims to check: lactational amenorrhoea acts at ovulation; periodic abstinence and withdrawal act at sperm meeting ovum; copper IUD acts at sperm motility (sperm stage) and fertilisation; hormonal IUD at implantation and cervix hostile to sperm; pill acts at ovulation, implantation and cervical mucus; surgical methods at passage of sperm or ovum.
- t13 ART flow: ICSI shown feeding the embryo stage that is then split into ZIFT (up to 8 blastomeres) and IUT (more than 8); GIFT and IUI shown as fertilisation inside the body. Check this grouping is acceptable.
- t09 sewage and biogas figures: "primary effluent" arrow and inoculum return follow the lesson text; the biogas plant is a generic floating-cover sketch, not a specific KVIC/IARI design.
- t08 food web: species (grass, grasshopper, mouse, rabbit, frog, snake, hawk) and links are my own illustrative example, not from the lesson. Check the trophic levels stated (snake T3 via mouse, T4 via frog).
