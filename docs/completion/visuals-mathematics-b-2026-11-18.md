## 4. Requested figures not drawn, and why
- Index numbers table (r08): the lesson does not teach index numbers, so there is nothing to illustrate.
- Compound vs simple interest curves (r12): the lesson does not cover simple vs compound interest as a topic; the compound growth is shown through the sinking fund figures instead.
- Confidence interval picture and Type I/II error table (r11): the lesson covers neither (it has estimation only as a named idea, and significance level alpha). Alpha appears as the tail area in the rejection-region figure.
- Slope field for 'applied' differential equations: r07 has no direction-field text; the figure was added because the brief asked for it and it supports the "family of curves" idea (see flagged item 1).
- Hexagon/other extras in r13 (the 2x + y <= 8, x + 2y <= 8 region example): similar in kind to the workshop figure, so not added.
- No lesson was left without a figure.

## 5. Needs subject-expert review
1. r07 direction-field figure: slope fields are not mentioned anywhere in the lesson text. Check that showing them (with the callout saying each curve of y = Ae^(x^2) runs along the grey slope marks) is acceptable at this syllabus depth.
2. r07 growth-decay: the decay panel uses y = 2e^(kx) with e^k = 1/2 (k = -ln 2, about -0.693); this is an illustrative choice, not a lesson example.
3. r08 trend-seasonal and cycle-irregular: all values (T = 100 + 2.5t, S = +10, -6, -12, +8; T = 50 + 2t, 6-year cycle, shock of -14 in year 4) are invented for illustration and labelled as such. Check the additive model with a sum-to-zero seasonal set is how the course presents it.
4. r08 least-squares figure: the vertical axis starts at 8 (stated in the figure) so that the gaps are visible; check this is acceptable.
5. r09 callout "(a,b) and (b,a) are separate cells, which is why 36 and not 21" repeats the lesson's own point.
6. r10 lp-special-cases is placed after the "many optimal solutions" solved problem, while the lesson's empty-region EXAMPLE (x + y <= 3 and x + y >= 7) comes earlier; the figure covers both.
7. r11 sampling-distribution figure draws the population as a normal curve (labelled schematic) only for illustration; the CLT statement for n >= 30 is the lesson's.
8. r11 t figure: critical values 2.306 (df 8) and 2.365 (df 7) were confirmed by numerical integration (each tail 0.025); they agree with the lesson's table.
9. r12 sinking-fund-timeline: amounts are rounded to the rupee and add to 4,99,999; the figure says so and shows "Sum approx 5,00,000". Check the rounding note is acceptable.
10. r12 flat-vs-reducing: total interest by the reducing method (about 6,619 = 12 x 8,884.88 - 1,00,000) is computed here and is not stated in the lesson.
11. r12 bond figure: the 6% value 1,053.46 and the curve are computed from the lesson's formula; the lesson only states the 10% value (950.26) and the discount/par/premium rule.
12. r12 EMI figure: monthly split (interest 1,000 in month 1, about 88 in month 12) is computed from the lesson's formula and is not in the lesson text.
13. r13 transport figure: the lesson's solution lists corners (30,0), (35,0), (35,5), (5,35), (0,35), (0,30); all six were reproduced exactly by the script, costs 250, 245, 260, 380, 385, 370 match.
