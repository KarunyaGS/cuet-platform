## 3. How PG admission works (quotes, with pages)

- Route by programme (p.2): "M.A., M.Sc., MCA, MPH, M.Tech. (except M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering & Electronics and Communication Engineering of School of Engineering), Post Graduate Diploma, Advanced Diploma Programmes will be through CUET (PG) 2026"; M.Sc. Biotechnology and M.Sc. "Computational Biology" through GAT-B score; MBA through CAT; M.Tech CSE and ECE through CCMT.
- Domain paper only (p.3, point 8): "Admission shall be given on the basis of performance of a candidate in Domain Knowledge paper only i.e. paper of concerned subject/s."
- Must sit the listed paper (p.3, point 4): "Students not appearing in the prescribed Subjects/Tests for these programmes shall not be admitted in these programmes."
- Merit plus deprivation points (p.3, point 5): "Admissions in these programmes shall be based on the performance of the candidates in CUET (PG)-2026 and deprivation points earned by the eligible candidates taken together." Point 6: no other deprivation points or weightage apply, and the points are in "Section V (Page 60)".
- No viva (p.7): "The candidates are admitted on merit based on their performance in the Computer Based Test (CBT) and the deprivation points added to their score ... No viva voce examination will be held for admission to these programmes." (MBA is different: CAT, GD and PI, 70/10/20, p.42.)
- Separate JNU application (pp.3, 7): candidates "must apply SEPARATELY after the declaration of CUET (PG) - 2026 result."
- Tie-breaks (p.7): higher CBT marks, then marks in the qualifying degree, then 10+2, then 10th; a candidate whose result is declared is preferred over one awaiting a result.
- Deprivation points (p.60): "up to a maximum of 12 points"; separate points per level (10th/12th, UG, PG) for candidates from Quartile 1 or 2 districts (the PG table shows 3+3 for Quartile 1 and 2+2 for Quartile 2; the cell text is small, so verify visually before quoting numbers); 5 points for Kashmiri migrants; 7 points for female/transgender candidates in SC/ST/OBC(NCL)/PWD/Quartile 1-2, 5 points for others. The note excludes GAT-B M.Sc. programmes, MBA, the School of Engineering M.Tech and DOP/Ph.D.
- Reservation (p.7): SC 15%, ST 7.5%, OBC (NCL) 27%, EWS 10%, PWD not less than 5% (horizontal).
- Preferences (p.93, f-h): up to three fields of study at the same level, one application form with order of preference; options are final; a candidate selected in a higher preference "will be offered admission only in that"; more than one application form for the same level leads to rejection of all.
- Seat blocking (p.94, n): "Selected candidates shall be required to block the seats, as per the instructions given in the offer letter within the scheduled time ... upload the required documents ... alongwith payment of prescribed fees in online payment mode." The schedules call this "Pre-enrolment registration and payment of fee with blocking of seats".
- Eligibility while awaiting results (p.93, XXII): admission only if the minimum percentage is secured and all documents including the final marksheet are submitted before the registration deadline.
- Reopened and repeat rounds (notices): see section 4. Candidates already admitted at their first preference cannot join the last-and-final call.
