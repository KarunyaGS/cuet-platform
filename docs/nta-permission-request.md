**Subject: Request for permission to reproduce and host question papers and answer keys of the Common University Entrance Test (CUET UG and CUET PG) on an educational platform**

Respected Sir / Madam,

1. **About us.** [Legal name] operates "CUET Prep" ([website address]), an online preparation platform for students appearing in CUET (UG) and CUET (PG). [One or two sentences: when it started, who runs it, whether it is free or paid.]

2. **What we do today.** We recognise that the question papers and answer keys of CUET are the property of the National Testing Agency. At present our platform only links to the files published on NTA's own websites (nta.ac.in, cuet.nta.nic.in and exams.nta.ac.in), names NTA as the source, and stores no copy of any NTA paper.

3. **What we request.** We request a non-exclusive permission to:

   (a) reproduce and host, on our platform, the CUET (UG) and CUET (PG) question papers and final answer keys that NTA has itself published, for the examinations of 2024, 2025 and 2026, and of later years as NTA releases them;

   (b) display those papers to registered students inside our platform, for reading, practice and download;

   (c) present the questions of those papers in typed, searchable form (for example as practice tests with the official answer key), without changing the wording, options, figures or answers of any question; and

   (d) present one language version of a bilingual paper on its own (for example the English version), with the question text unchanged, so that students can study in their chosen medium.

   A list of the papers concerned, with NTA's own file addresses, is enclosed as Annexure A.

4. **Undertakings.** If permission is granted, we undertake that:

   (a) every paper and every question will carry a clear acknowledgement: "Source: National Testing Agency (NTA). Reproduced with permission.", with the examination, year and, where NTA states them, the date and shift;

   (b) no question, option, figure or answer will be altered; any error we notice will be reported to NTA and not corrected silently;

   (c) we will not state or imply that our platform is run, approved or endorsed by NTA, and will not use NTA's name or emblem except in the acknowledgement above;

   (d) we will not sell the papers as such, nor pass them to any third party for re-publication;

   (e) we will remove or correct any material within [7] working days of a written request from NTA; and

   (f) we will follow any further condition NTA may lay down, including any fee or licence terms.

5. **Linking.** We also request confirmation that we may continue to link to the files published on NTA's websites, with NTA named as the source, as we do now.

6. **Clarification sought.** If NTA has a standing policy, licence or fee for the reuse of its question papers, we would be grateful to be directed to it. If permission can be granted only in part (for example for hosting but not for typed reproduction, or for certain years only), we request that the permitted part be stated.

We would be glad to provide any further information, or to meet the concerned officer. Kindly quote our reference number in your reply.
