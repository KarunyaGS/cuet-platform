**Date:** 12 November 2026  
**Where it was run:** on a throwaway copy of the local database, now deleted. **The local database itself has not been changed by this import**, and neither has the live database.

## Why this was a trial and not the real import

The corrected syllabi arrive as "not reviewed". Students see only syllabi that are machine-checked or approved by a reviewer. So importing the corrections does not unhide papers; it hides every corrected paper that is visible today until a reviewer approves it. My earlier statement that the import "unhides 102 papers" was wrong.

## What the trial import did

- **Replaced with the corrected version:** 151 papers (the old versions are archived, not deleted).
- **Left as they are:** 42 papers the review confirmed as correct, including Physics.
- **Refused:** LAQP21 (Lepcha). Its review holds no transcribed rows.
- **One correction needed a limit raised:** a row in MTQP03 has a 1,002-character title; the import limit was 1,000 and is now 2,000.
- **Chemistry (SCQP08):** fixed before the trial. Unit-I to Unit-III under two headings stay units (6 rows), as printed on PDF pages 3 to 5.
